# Glossary — terms and target numbers

Every target the course sets, in one table. Where the source is ambiguous it says so; where the
figure is an unevidenced assertion it is marked `(claimed)`.

## The gates

| Gate | Test | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mockup test | < $1.00 cost per link click, on ≥5 link clicks |
| 2 | Design test | < $1.00 CPC, 3-5 link clicks, 2-3 winners per ~100 designs |
| 3 | Catalog scaling | catalog-wide CPC under $1.00 |
| 4 | Conversion | ROAS 2.0+ sustained 2 consecutive days |

## Ad metrics

| Term | What it means here | Target |
|---|---|---|
| CPC | Cost per *link click* — not per click on the whole ad | under $1.00 to pass a gate; $0.50-0.75 steady state |
| ROAS | Revenue ÷ ad spend | 2.0 to unlock scaling; 2.3-3.0 steady state; ~1.9 is breakeven |
| Trailing 7-day ROAS | The number the budget bands read | see the band table in [playbook](playbook.md) |
| Link clicks | Minimum sample before judging a mockup | ≥5 (1-3 is noise) |
| Test window | Thursday morning → Sunday 11:55pm | ~4 days, ~$50 |
| Test budget | Per ad set, both mockup and design tests | $12.50/day |

## Vocabulary

- Mockup test — testing the *photo*, holding the design constant.
- Design test — testing the *designs*, holding the winning mockup constant.
- Reskin — regenerating the whole catalog onto the winning mockup style (~4-5 hours per 100 products).
- Brand why — the identity reason a customer buys; stage 1's real deliverable.
- Flywheel — reinvesting excess profit into the next week's ad spend.
- Sunset — retiring unengaged subscribers to protect deliverability.
