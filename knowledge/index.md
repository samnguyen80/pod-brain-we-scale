## Reading the numbers

Figures tagged `(claimed)` are the instructors' assertions with no evidence shown — mostly revenue
screenshots and survey statistics. Several case-study numbers contradict themselves on camera; the
source cards flag each one rather than smoothing it over. The operational thresholds (budgets, CPC
gates, ROAS bands, margin targets) are the durable part.
