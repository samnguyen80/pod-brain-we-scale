# Design volume

The load-bearing belief of the course: volume is the strategy, not a substitute for one.

## The claim

A survey of 10,000+ sellers is cited as showing over 52% run 250-500 live designs, and that
everyone past 250 designs had crossed $10K in sales `(claimed)`. No methodology is shown. Treat it
as the course's organising assumption rather than evidence.

## The volume ladder

| Milestone | Designs |
|---|---|
| Launch | 100 |
| First 2.0 ROAS | 150-250 |
| $10K+/month sellers | 250-500 `(claimed)` |
| Weekly cadence while scaling | 25 |
| Weekly cadence at steady state | 25-50 |

## The hit rates

- 5-10% of designs become winners
- Roughly 1 winner per 20-30 designs tested `(claimed)`
- Roughly 1 winner per 20 static ads tested
- "Top 1% of sellers" produce 7 designs/day `(claimed)`

These rates are the actual argument for volume. If one design in 25 wins, then a 10-design store
isn't a small business — it is a business that has statistically not yet started. That reframing is
the most useful idea in stage 2, independent of whether the survey is real.

## Why CPC falls as the catalog grows

The mechanism the course sells: more designs means more chances for Meta's optimiser to find a
match between a person and a joke, so cost per click drops. The flagship case claims $5.00 CPC in
January falling to $0.37 at 2.3 ROAS as the catalog went 100 → 150 → 200 `(claimed)`.

Expect 2-4 weekly cycles to bring catalog-wide CPC under $1.

## The practical constraint

Research is capped at a 2-hour timer and 10-15 saved references — the failure mode at this stage is
analysis paralysis, not insufficient inspiration. Production specs are fixed so volume doesn't
degrade quality: 5000x5000px export, 1-3 colours per design, 6-8 garment colours.

## The counter-consideration

Volume without a coherent brand why produces a catalog of unrelated jokes that no repeat customer
has a reason to return to. Stage 1 exists to prevent exactly that, and the email stage depends on
it. Volume compounds only if the designs belong to the same identity.
