# Conversion diagnosis

Once clicks are cheap, the store becomes the constraint. The course gives a strict diagnostic order
so you fix one thing at a time instead of redesigning everything at once.

## The three funnel rates

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Product page works | add-to-cart rate | 6-8% |
| Cart works | reach-checkout rate | 5-6% |
| Checkout works | sessions converted | 2.5-3% |

Read them in order. The first rate that misses its target is the one you fix — a bad add-to-cart
rate makes every downstream number meaningless, because the traffic never got far enough to test
the cart.

## The three-number green light

You may move on to scaling when all three hold simultaneously:

- CPC consistently under $1.00
- conversion rate above 2.5%
- AOV above $45

The source states the AOV figure with a verbal self-correction on camera; $45 is the intended
number but the card for 4.8 flags the ambiguity.

## Fix the worst gap first

The course works three examples, and the pattern is always "compare each number to target, fix the
biggest relative gap, change nothing else":

| Situation | Weakest link | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| $0.78 CPC · 1.8% CR · $42 AOV | conversion rate | the store, not the ads |
| ~$0.65 CPC · 3.1% CR · $28 AOV | AOV | bundles, thresholds, upsells |
| $1.25 CPC · 2.9% CR · $45 AOV | CPC | back to creative and catalog |

The discipline matters more than the specific fix: changing three things at once means learning
nothing from the result.

## Why AOV is usually the answer

Early-stage AOV sits at $30-35 because people buy one shirt. Every lever that lifts it — the $74.99
free-shipping threshold, bundles, post-purchase upsells — works on customers the ads have already
paid for, which is why it drops almost entirely to the bottom line. See [[unit-economics]].

Check the Printify multi-item shipping default before anything else; it quietly penalises the exact
orders you are trying to encourage.

## The gate out

ROAS 2.0+ sustained for two consecutive days. Never raise budget before it. Typical catalog size
when a brand first gets here is 150-250 designs, which is a reminder that conversion problems are
often really catalog problems.
