# 1.1 Launch Your Store in Minutes

Stage: 1) Branding
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hduC-C8pBpE&t=416s
Transcript: [../../transcripts/1-1-launch-your-store-in-minutes.md](../../transcripts/1-1-launch-your-store-in-minutes.md)

## The job of this lesson
A merchant should walk away with a live Shopify store, a connected Printify fulfillment account, and a POD-optimized theme + starter pages already installed — all set up in minutes via a free tool called BuildMyPod.ai, instead of the old multi-week manual setup process.

## Playbook
1. Pick your sales channel: Shopify (own store), not a marketplace — needed for full control over branding/checkout. `[00:07:30]`
2. Pick a fulfillment model: print-on-demand (vs. dropshipping or 3PL) so you never hold inventory; the instructor's chosen POD partner is Printify, over Printful/Gelato, for its combination of shipping speed, print quality, and price. `[00:08:22]`
3. Go to buildmypod.ai, sign up with email/password, confirm your email. `[00:11:04]`
4. In BuildMyPod, pick a starting niche from the preset list (e.g. "nature and outdoors") — treat this as a placeholder starting point you can swap later, not a final decision. `[00:11:39]`
5. Choose 2 homepage banner options offered by the tool (swappable later). `[00:12:01]`
6. Click "Launch Shopify and sign up" inside BuildMyPod: sign up for Shopify's free 3-day trial, then $1/month for the next 3 months; enter email + card, choose the Basic Shopify plan, skip the business address ("add later"), answer the onboarding survey (online store, new business, selling print-on-demand products), and enter a placeholder brand name for now. `[00:12:26]`
7. Bookmark the new Shopify admin URL; create a dedicated folder (e.g. "business") to store all brand assets/logins going forward. `[00:13:28]`
8. Back in BuildMyPod, click "I have created my account" to confirm activation, then click Done. `[00:13:58]`
9. Install the free BuildMyPod app from the Shopify App Store (this is what pushes the theme, pages, and Printify products into the store); decline the paid upsell (100 extra products + branded customer-service GPT + 50 Meta ad templates + updated theme) — the course teaches you to build these yourself for free. `[00:14:30]`
10. Create a Printify account: click "create a Printify account" → "Get started for free" → sign up with email → complete the onboarding form (selling products, new to online selling, side hustle, individual seller, selling in US). `[00:15:32]`
11. Back in BuildMyPod, click Done, then integrate BuildMyPod with Printify (click through, then "Allow") so Printify can talk to Shopify. `[00:16:07]`
12. Install the Printify app on your Shopify store — critically, make sure you install it onto the *new* store you just created (not a pre-existing Shopify/Printify account), then log in with your Printify credentials inside the app. `[00:16:48]`
13. Back in BuildMyPod, click Done — the store is now live with a POD-optimized theme and Printify auto-uploading niche-matched placeholder products. `[00:18:15]`
14. Remove Shopify's default password-protection when ready (left for later in the training). `[00:18:15]`
15. Bookmark both Shopify admin and Printify dashboard for repeated reference going forward. `[00:19:14]`
16. Optional: redeem the Printify discount code "wescale" for a free month of Printify Premium — save this for Section 4 (once sales are coming in) rather than using it immediately. `[00:19:14]`

- Hiring someone to do the setup instead: $500–$2,000 `[00:09:46]` (claimed)
- BuildMyPod setup time: about 5 minutes vs. weeks manually `[00:09:46]`
- Shopify trial terms via BuildMyPod: free for 3 days, then $1/month for the following 3 months `[00:12:26]`
- Time saved estimate: this section replaces what was "3 to 4 hours at least" in the previous version of the course `[00:19:59]` (claimed)
- Printify discount: 1 free month of Printify Premium, code "wescale" `[00:19:14]`

## Tools and services named
- Shopify — the storefront/e-commerce platform, hosts checkout and theme — Basic plan, $1/mo for first 3 months via trial offer
- Printify — POD fulfillment partner (printing + shipping); chosen over Printful and Gelato — free to start, 1 free month Premium via code
- BuildMyPod.ai — free tool that auto-sets up Shopify account, POD-optimized theme, standard pages (About/Contact), and Printify product sync — free tier used in course; paid upsell tier also exists (bundle of 100 products + CS GPT + ad templates + extra theme)
- Printify app (Shopify App Store) — connects Printify catalog/fulfillment to the Shopify store
- BuildMyPod app (Shopify App Store) — applies theme, pages, and product sync from BuildMyPod

## Watch-outs
- When installing the Printify app, make sure it's being installed onto the *newly created* store, not an old/pre-existing Shopify or Printify account — easy to mix up if you already had either account before. `[00:16:48]`
- Store is password-protected by default (Shopify's default) — this is expected and handled later, not a setup error. `[00:18:15]`
- The niche, banners, and starter products from BuildMyPod are placeholders meant to speed up setup — not final creative decisions.
- Support is available 24/7 human support from BuildMyPod if any setup step seems off — use it rather than getting stuck. `[00:19:38]`
- Dollar figures ($157K sales, cost comparisons) are instructor claims, not verified.
