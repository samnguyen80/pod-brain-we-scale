## The job of this lesson
A merchant should leave this short closing lesson understanding that growing from $10K to $800K+ is not a new playbook — it's the exact same system from the course, rinsed and repeated with discipline — and knowing to return to the scaling roadmap (5.5) whenever they hit a wall.

## Playbook
1. Treat "$10K to $800K" as the same system repeated, not a search for new tactics — the instructor frames this as ruthless, singular focus on one system, the way top founders stay focused on one idea for an extended period `[11:57:18]`–`[11:57:47]`.
2. Accept that true mastery of every concept in the course only comes from doing it yourself — running your own tests, making your own ad and design decisions — not from having watched the training `[11:57:47]`–`[11:58:13]`.
3. When you hit a wall, go back to the scaling roadmap (5.5) — not just to pick the next action, but to build pattern recognition over time (e.g. noticing that launching a batch of designs reliably moves sales) `[11:58:13]`–`[11:58:40]`.
4. Keep running financials daily — named as one of the two most important, and personally hardest-won, skills in the whole course `[11:58:40]`–`[11:59:07]`.

## Numbers and thresholds
None introduced in this lesson — it is a wrap-up with no new figures.

## Watch-outs
- The instructor's own admitted failure mode before building this system: years of running the business reactively, "like a chicken with his head cut off," in constant firefighting mode with no repeatable process `[11:59:07]`–`[11:59:19]`.
