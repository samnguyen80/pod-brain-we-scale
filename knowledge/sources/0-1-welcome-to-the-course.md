## The job of this lesson
This is the pitch/orientation lesson. A merchant should walk away understanding what "branded print on demand" (POD) is as a category (vs. marketplace selling), why this specific window of opportunity exists, and the 5-part roadmap the rest of the course follows — enough to commit to going through the full course.

## Playbook
1. Understand the category being taught: branded POD = your own Shopify store + POD fulfillment, not selling on Etsy/Amazon Merch/Redbubble marketplaces. `[00:00:28]`
2. Internalize why marketplaces are getting harder: AI is flooding Etsy/Amazon with competitors fighting over the same shared marketplace audience; a branded store lets you build your own separate audience instead of splitting theirs. `[00:01:27]`
3. Treat the course as full and free, structured around one real case-brand ("Sloth Hiking Club") that the instructors expose in detail throughout — use it as a living example, not just a fictional demo. `[00:05:13]`
4. Hold the 5 fundamentals as the mental model for the whole course, in order: (1) brand, (2) products, (3) store, (4) marketing, (5) scaling. `[00:06:05]`
5. Expect different paths to work — full-time-job side hustle, all-in full-time dive, or a hybrid — the instructors each took a different one; pick the pace that fits your situation rather than assuming one "correct" way.
