# 2.4 Create Mockups That Sell

Stage: 2) Design
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hduC-C8pBpE&t=7571s
Transcript: [../../transcripts/2-4-create-mockups-that-sell.md](../../transcripts/2-4-create-mockups-that-sell.md)

## The job of this lesson
A merchant should walk away able to generate 10 professional, Facebook-feed-optimized flatlay mockups for a t-shirt design using the WeScale Mockup Gem, understand the anatomy of a compelling mockup (shirt + background + props), and know which mockup styles/mistakes to avoid before testing on paid traffic.

## Playbook
1. Understand mockup anatomy: shirt + background + props combined (the design itself is placed on top separately); think of a mockup like a movie poster — it must communicate vibe, tone, and quality instantly, not just show the product. `[02:06:42]`
2. Study bad-mockup patterns to avoid: t-shirt looks "floating" with no shadow/weight, design off-center or too small to read (especially on mobile), too many distracting props, background unrelated to the product, main subject not the visual focus. `[02:07:46]`
3. Study good-mockup patterns: t-shirt fills most of the frame, minimal/purposeful props, real shadowing/lighting so the shirt looks like it's resting on a surface, nothing obstructing the print area, clean background. Download the free "good vs bad mockup" PDF guide from the WeScale community as a reference. `[02:09:40]`, `[02:10:37]`
4. Set up split-screen: WeScale Mockup Gem (prompt generation) in one tab, image generator in the other, same technique used for design generation. Prompt the Gem simply with "[niche] mockup" — it returns pre-built prompts already tuned for winning specs (1080x1080, 1:1 square ratio, professional realistic flatlay, top-down perspective, proven colors). `[02:11:04]`
5. Generate each prompt's image, review against the good/bad checklist, and reject ones with distracting elements (e.g. an oversized dark object crowding a corner) or off-brand backgrounds (e.g. a beach scene for a golf niche). `[02:12:57]`
6. Direct-edit prompts in-chat for specific needs: add requested props ("I want a mockup for the [niche] with a [prop] in it"), or remove a styling detail by deleting the offending sentence from the prompt text (e.g. removing "the bottom hem is casually knotted on one side" to un-knot a shirt for a male-skewing niche — knotted hems tend to perform better for feminine niches). `[02:16:02]`
7. Repeat until 10 usable mockup variations are generated and downloaded into the brand's folder, then finish each in Canva: resize/reposition so the shirt properly fills the frame before adding to the store. `[02:19:38]`
8. Choose flatlay only — no lifestyle/model shots, hanger shots, or elaborate staging — based on WeScale's internal testing across 600+ community members showing flatlay converts best; the goal is letting the customer picture themselves in the shirt, not look at someone else wearing it. `[02:20:40]`
9. Pause here and don't proceed until all 10 mockup variations exist — one becomes the template mockup style applied across all 100 products later. `[02:22:23]`

## Numbers and thresholds
- 10 mockups made "in about 15 minutes" using these tools `[02:06:15]` (claimed)

## Watch-outs
- Avoid: obvious stock photos, lifestyle/model mockups, overly cluttered backgrounds, bland colors, shirts that render too small in frame. `[02:20:40]`
- Realism matters — look for natural fabric wrinkles, correct lighting/shadow, print that looks "printed, not pasted," and design placement centered on the chest. `[02:20:40]`
- Reject off-brand/off-niche outputs (e.g. a beach background for a golf mockup) even if the image itself is technically well-made. `[02:15:04]`
- The Gem sometimes bakes in styling defaults (like a knotted hem) that don't fit the target demographic — check and edit the prompt text directly rather than regenerating blind. `[02:16:02]`
- Free community support (WeScale GPT, 21-day challenge structure) has a ceiling — deeper feedback/accountability requires the paid WeScale Accelerator; verify current pricing/terms independently. `[02:22:23]`
