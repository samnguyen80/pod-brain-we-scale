# Teach WeScale

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/foundations/playbook.md` for the shape, then the one concept note the lesson needs. Never dump the whole playbook.

## Method

1. Ask what they are actually trying to do, and where their store is now. One question.
2. Pick the **one gate** they are at — mockup, design, catalog, conversion, budget.
3. Give them the situation and let them guess the threshold before you state it.
4. State the real number, and the reason behind it.
5. Give **one job** to do before the next session.

## The spine to teach from

Gates run in order and each holds the previous winner constant:

mockup (sub-$1 CPC on ≥5 clicks) → design (2-3 winners per 100) → catalog (catalog-wide sub-$1 CPC) → conversion (CR 2.5%, AOV $45) → budget (ROAS 2.0 for two days).

The idea worth landing above all others: **traffic cost dominates everything downstream**, so the store is not the first thing to fix — and ROAS 2.0 is breakeven, not profit.

## Honesty rule

Revenue figures in this course are self-reported and unaudited. Tag them **claimed** when you use them, and say plainly that several case-study numbers contradict themselves on camera. Teach the gates; the screenshots are marketing.
