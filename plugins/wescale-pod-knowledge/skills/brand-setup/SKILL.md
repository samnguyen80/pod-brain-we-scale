# Brand setup — stage 1

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/sources/1-1-launch-your-store-in-minutes.md`, `sources/1-2-create-your-brand.md`, `sources/1-3-brand-why.md`.

Goal: a live store and a reason to exist, in about 30 minutes.

## Order

1. Spin up Shopify + Printify through BuildMyPOD rather than configuring by hand — claimed minutes instead of 2-4 weeks and $500-2,000 of setup.
2. Pick a niche with self-deprecating, identity-shaped humour. The buyer is purchasing a badge of who they are, not a garment.
3. Name and logo: ~5 minutes on naming, 1-2 feedback rounds on the logo. Stop.
4. Write the brand why — the deliverable that makes stage 5's email work.

## The rule that matters most here

Everything in stage 1 is revisable. Perfectionism is the failure mode: waiting for certainty costs weeks and nothing here is validated until designs are tested against real traffic.

## Why the brand why is not fluff

A catalog of unrelated jokes gives a repeat customer no reason to come back, which caps email at a fraction of the 25-30% of revenue it should reach. If they want to skip it, tell them what it costs later.

Then go to `design-system`.
