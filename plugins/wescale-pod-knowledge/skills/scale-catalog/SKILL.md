# Scale the catalog — gate 3

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/concepts/design-volume.md` and `sources/4-7-scaling-your-catalog.md`.

## The rates that justify volume

- 5-10% of designs become winners; roughly **1 winner per 20-30 tested** (claimed).
- Cadence: **25 new designs/week** while scaling, 25-50 at steady state.
- Expect **2-4 weekly cycles** to bring catalog-wide CPC under $1.
- Catalog size when 2.0 ROAS typically first appears: **150-250 designs**.

The reframing that matters: if one design in 25 wins, a 10-design store has not statistically started yet.

## The mechanism (and its limit)

More designs give Meta's optimiser more chances to match a person to a joke, so CPC falls. The flagship case claims $5.00 CPC falling to $0.37 at 2.3 ROAS as the catalog went 100 → 150 → 200 (claimed).

Volume only compounds if the designs belong to **one identity**. A catalog of unrelated jokes gives a repeat customer no reason to return, and kills the email stage later. If their brand why is vague, route to `brand-setup` first.

## Do

Give a weekly design number and the next review date. Root fix for almost every plateau is more and better designs — not structural or targeting tweaks.
