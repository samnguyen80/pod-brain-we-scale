# Design system — stage 2

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/sources/2-1-design-research.md`, `sources/2-2-create-winning-designs.md`, `sources/2-3-scale-to-100-designs.md`, `sources/2-4-create-mockups-that-sell.md`. Volume doctrine is in `concepts/design-volume.md`.

Goal: **100 designs**, not 10.

## Research — capped

60-minute timer, 30-50 proven designs reviewed per niche, screenshot **10-15** to an inspiration bank, write down **3 shared patterns**, stop. Cap the whole stage at 2 hours. Saving 100+ references causes paralysis, not insight.

## Design principles

Simple, readable from 10 feet, one message, text-heavy tests better than graphic-only, **1-3 colours** max.

## Production specs

- Export **5000x5000px** for correct print DPI.
- **6-8 garment colours** per design (tested against 3 and against 14).
- Mockups **1080x1080**, 1:1, ~10 variations, max 2-3 supporting props.

## Watch-outs

Trademark and copyright risk is real — do not ship someone else's IP, brand names, or protected phrases. Check AI output for the tell-tale artefacts that mark it as slop before it goes live.

Then go to `mockup-test`.
